Review of Systems in Pediatrics

Third Stage

Dr. Moustafa Ali Alshammari

Pediatric Nursing

2General

General

Overall state of health; weight loss/gain (compare with growth charts); obesity; delays in physical, psychosocial, or language development; fatigue; weakness; malaise; difficulty with ADLs; chronic pain.

3Integument

Integument

Skin texture, birthmarks, pigmentation; color changes (jaundice, cyanosis, mottling, pallor, bruising, petechiae); rashes, lesions; dryness; pruritus; hives; dermatitis; seborrhea; acne; mole changes; hair/nail disorders; pediculosis.

4Head

Head

Size and shape (including fontanels); microcephaly, macrocephaly; injuries; headaches; dizziness.

5Eyes

Eyes

Visual problems (blurred, double vision, strabismus, photophobia); behaviors (sitting close to screen, squinting, rubbing eyes); corrective lenses; last exam; drainage; infections; tearing; eyelid edema.

6Ears

Ears

Infections (otitis media/externa); ear pain; drainage; surgery (tympanostomy tubes); delayed speech; hearing loss; last hearing exam; tinnitus.

7Nose

Nose

Frequent rhinorrhea; URIs; sinusitis; allergic rhinitis; congestion; allergies; mouth breathing; snoring; OSA; epistaxis; allergic salute/crease; dark eye circles; altered smell.

8Mouth/Throat

Mouth/Throat

Tooth eruption/loss; number of teeth; teething; dental care/fluoride; caries; first/last dental visit; pain; trauma; sores; candida; gum bleeding; tonsillar enlargement; tonsillectomy; postnasal drip; cleft lip/palate; sore throats; strep; dysphagia; hoarseness; abnormal cry.

9Chest & Neck

Chest & Neck

Chest: Pain, newborn breast enlargement, gynecomastia, lesions, discharge. Neck: Limited ROM, pain, stiffness, torticollis, thyroid enlargement, lymphadenopathy.

10Lymphatics

Lymphatics

Frequent infections, fevers, node pain/swelling/tenderness, hepatosplenomegaly, chills, night sweats, pallor.

11Respiratory

Respiratory

Frequent infections, dyspnea, apnea, stridor, croup, pneumonia, CF, asthma, wheezing, chronic cough, sputum, exercise intolerance, cyanosis, TB exposure, last chest X-ray, smoke exposure.

12Cardiovascular

Cardiovascular

Murmurs, congenital heart disease, poor feeding/weight gain, chest pain, cyanosis, tachypnea, tachycardia, hypertension, edema, cold extremities, palpitations, syncope, exercise intolerance, rheumatic fever, anemia, transfusion history.

13Gastrointestinal

Gastrointestinal

Appetite, intolerances, diet; abdominal pain; ulcers; nausea; vomiting; reflux; bowel changes; GI infections; diarrhea; flatulence; constipation; stool description; laxative use; blood in stool; jaundice; anal itching; fissures; pinworms.

14Genitourinary

Genitourinary

Dysuria, frequency, urgency, enuresis; UTIs; hematuria; polyuria/oliguria; congenital anomalies (epispadias, hypospadias, cryptorchidism, hydrocele, hernia); surgeries; testicular self-exam (adolescents); STIs; sexual activity.

15Gynecologic (Adolescent females)

Gynecologic (Adolescent females)

Age of menarche, LMP, cycle length/duration, dysmenorrhea, heavy bleeding, discharge, abnormal bleeding, sexual activity, contraception, last Pap smear, obstetric history if relevant.

16Musculoskeletal

Musculoskeletal

Activity level, weakness, clumsiness, ataxia, lack of coordination, unusual movements; joint/muscle pain, cramps, swelling, sprains, fractures, mobility issues, stiffness, abnormal gait, limps, spinal curvatures.

17Neurologic

Neurologic

Affect, mood, ataxia, tremors, tics, dizziness, syncope, tingling, sensory changes, paresthesia, seizures, LOC, memory loss, headaches, aphasia, speech problems, fears, nightmares, unusual habits.

18Endocrine

Endocrine

Growth disturbances, obesity, polyuria, polyphagia, polydipsia, thyroid disease, type 1/2 diabetes, excessive sweating, salty skin, temperature intolerance, precocious/delayed puberty, hair distribution changes.

19Hematologic

Hematologic

Anemia, pallor, lymphadenopathy, bleeding disorders, bruising, petechiae, gum bleeding, transfusions, toxic exposures, radiation, chemotherapy.

20Psychosocial Development

Psychosocial Development

Delays, behavior changes, tantrums, breath-holding spells, enuresis, school failure, withdrawal, irritability, sleep problems, depression, substance abuse, eating disorders, psychiatric disorders.
